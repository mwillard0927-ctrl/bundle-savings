How these numbers work
  • Bundle values are locked. Each item's share of $379.90 comes from the deal-based split in the first comparison, so it reflects how the items are valued relative to each other. Price updates and button presses never change these numbers. The breakfast sausage slot is worth $11.42 whether you pick links or patties.
  • How each item's bundle value was figured. Cattleman's sells the bundle for one price, $379.90, with no price per item. To give each meat a value, the first comparison priced every item at the best local deal found that week. Each item then got the same share of $379.90 as its share of that deal-priced total. For example, the sirloin was about a quarter of the deal-priced total, so it gets about a quarter of the bundle price ($99.90). The bundle's overall discount or markup is spread evenly across every item rather than landing on one or two of them.
  • Why these values are reasonable. Deal prices are what a careful shopper actually pays, so the split follows real store prices instead of guesses. Pricier cuts like sirloin and stew carry more of the bundle's value, and cheap items like leg quarters carry less, the same way they would at the store. The values are still estimates: they come from one week's deals, and Cattleman's own per-item cost could split a little differently. They're locked so the value-left numbers stay steady and comparable over time.
  • Gauges drain 1 lb per tap. Value left is the locked value times the share of pounds left. Spare ribs count by pound (3 slabs, about 9 lb). Use "+1 lb" to undo a mis-tap. Breakfast has a Links/Patties switch inside its button; its store price stays blank until one is picked. Each person's gauges, sausage pick, store picks and Moo/Kake choice save to their own account when signed in, so two people never share a freezer. Signed out, they save on that device only.
  • Michigan prices only. Every price was read on Oct 19, 2026 from each store's own website, set to the stores near Moo: Kroger (28250 Dequindre Rd, Warren), Meijer (29505 Mound Rd, Warren), Walmart (29176 Van Dyke Ave, Warren), ALDI (29475 Mound Rd, Warren, in-store prices), Sam's Club (31020 John R Rd, Madison Heights) and BJ's (29101 John R Rd, Madison Heights). Costco doesn't post its warehouse meat prices online, so it has no prices yet.
  • Store buttons all start off. Press a store to add its regular shelf prices; each row shows the cheapest price among the stores you have on. BJ's, Costco and Sam's Club are membership stores (memberships not included). Sale prices, Walmart rollbacks and ALDI price cuts count as deals, not regular prices. Package-priced sausage is extended at its per-pound rate to 3 lb.
  • Deals uses only the original deals that were confirmed in a Michigan ad or on a Warren store's page: Meijer 80% lean ground beef $4.79/lb (Oct 21–27), Walmart chicken breast rollback $2.23/lb, Meijer assorted bone-in chops $1.79/lb and spare ribs $2.79/lb (Oct 14–20), and Meijer blade steak $2.99/lb (no end date posted). Kroger's $8.99 petite sirloin was dropped: Kroger's Oct 21–27 Michigan ad isn't posted yet and the Warren Kroger doesn't list petite sirloin. Other current deals show only in the store tables. An item keeps its regular price when no deal beats it.
  • Moo and Kake switch whose home the driving starts from. Each uses the closest location of each store. Prices stay the ones from the Warren-area stores; the same chain usually prices the same nearby, but Walmart can differ a little from store to store.
  • Driving uses Moo's 2004 Honda Accord (about 24 mpg) or Kake's 2014 GMC Terrain (about 26 mpg), metro Detroit regular gas at $4.54/gal (AAA, Oct 19, 2026), plus about 10¢ a mile for wear (AAA's maintenance estimate). Distances are straight-line miles times 1.3 for the road grid, at 28 mph. Regular prices are one round trip through every store; with Deals on, each store is its own round trip, since deals run on different dates. Cattleman's is counted as free to reach.
  • Potatoes adds a 10 lb bag of russet potatoes to the store side at the cheapest regular price among the stores you have on, so the bundle's savings go up by that amount. Its store joins the drive when the car is on.

Sources: Kroger Universal Mall, Kroger weekly ad, Meijer weekly ads, Walmart Warren Supercenter, ALDI, Sam's Club Madison Heights, BJ's Madison Heights, Costco, AAA gas prices, AAA driving costs.
